10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Drive to Branson, Missouri

All three families drive from Plano, Ashburn, and Milton to Branson. Check into hotel and rest.

Day 2: Silver Dollar City

Spend the day at Silver Dollar City with rides, shows, food, and family fun.

Day 3: Stage Drama — David

Attend the stage show David in Branson.

Day 4: Silver Dollar City Again

Return to Silver Dollar City for another relaxed day at the park.

Day 5: Mountain Golf Cart Ride

Enjoy a mountain golf cart ride in the Branson area.

Day 6: Drive to St. Louis

All families drive together in their own cars from Branson to St. Louis. Spend the night in St. Louis.

Day 7: St. Louis Attraction + Drive to Chicago

Visit the Cathedral Basilica of Saint Louis or another major attraction, then drive to Chicago and spend the night.

Day 8: Navy Pier + Downtown Chicago + Boat Ride + Halal Dinner

Spend the day at Navy Pier, explore downtown Chicago, take a boat or ferry ride, and enjoy halal dinner at D1.

Day 9: Lake Michigan Beach Day

Relax at a beach on Lake Michigan and enjoy the final full day together.

Day 10: Return Home

Salman and Sadia return to Milton, Naveed and Sadia return to Ashburn, and Luqman and Munazza return to Plano.
