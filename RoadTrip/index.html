10-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Drive to Branson, Missouri

All three families drive from Plano, Ashburn, and Milton to Branson. Check into hotel and rest.

Driving Time

FamilyRouteApprox. Drive Time
Luqman Munazza FamilyPlano → Branson8 hours
Naveed Sadia FamilyAshburn → Branson15–16 hours
Salman Sadia FamilyMilton → Branson13–14 hours

Day 2: Silver Dollar City

Spend the day at Silver Dollar City with rides, shows, food, and family fun.

Day 3: Stage Drama — David

Attend the stage show David in Branson.

Day 4: Silver Dollar City Again

Return to Silver Dollar City for another relaxed day at the park.

Day 5: Mountain Golf Cart Ride

Enjoy a mountain golf cart ride in the Branson area.

Day 6: Drive to St. Louis

All families drive together in their own cars from Branson to St. Louis. Spend the night in St. Louis.

Driving Time

All families: Branson → St. Louis — approximately 4 hours.

Day 7: St. Louis Attraction + Travel to Chicago

Visit the Cathedral Basilica of Saint Louis, Gateway Arch, Forest Park, or another major St. Louis attraction, then travel to Chicago and spend the night.

Driving Time

All families: St. Louis → Chicago — approximately 4.5–5 hours.

Day 8: Chicago — Navy Pier, Downtown, Boat Ride, Halal Dinner

Spend the day at Navy Pier, explore downtown Chicago, take a boat or ferry ride, and enjoy halal dinner.

Day 9: Lake Michigan Beach and Another Halal Dinner

Relax at Lake Michigan beach, enjoy the lakefront, and finish the day with another halal dinner together.

Day 10: Return Home

Families begin their return journeys home after a memorable trip together.
